Caveman Key is a keyboard for iPhone that rewrites whatever you've typed into
caveman speech, in any app. Type as usual, switch to Caveman Key with 🌐, and
tap **Cavemanify**.

## Getting started

1. Open the Caveman Key app and follow the setup steps, or go to **Settings ›
   General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard…** and choose
   **Caveman Key**.
2. In any text field, hold or tap 🌐 to switch to Caveman Key.
3. Tap **Cavemanify**. Tap again for another voice, or hold the key and slide
   to the voice you want. **Revert** puts your own words back.

## Requirements

Caveman Key needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple
Intelligence turned on in Settings. Without it, the keyboard can't rewrite
anything.

## Privacy

Everything happens on your iPhone, and nothing you type leaves it. See the
[privacy policy](/caveman-key/privacy/).
