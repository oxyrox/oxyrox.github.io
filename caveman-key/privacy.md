*Last updated: 27 September 2026*

Caveman Key doesn't collect, store, or share any of your data.

**What you type stays on your iPhone.** The keyboard rewrites your text with
Apple's on-device model, Apple Intelligence. Your text is not sent to us or to
anyone else, and nothing in Caveman Key connects to the internet.

**No Full Access.** The keyboard never asks for "Allow Full Access", so iOS
keeps it from reaching the network at all.

**Settings stay on your phone.** Choices you make in the app, such as the voice
a first tap uses, are stored on your iPhone and shared only between the Caveman
Key app and its keyboard.

**No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.**

**Crash reports.** If you have agreed in iOS Settings to share analytics with
app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These contain no
text you have typed. You can turn this off in **Settings › Privacy & Security ›
Analytics & Improvements**.

**Changes.** If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here
with a new date.

**Questions:** see the [Caveman Key support page](/caveman-key/).
